Learn: radiation
What is it?
Energetic particles from the Sun and from outside the solar system. Outside Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere, astronauts receive much more of it than on the ground.
How it's measured in space
Dosimeters on the spacecraft record the dose rate continuously. Crew also carry personal dosimeters.
Why it matters
Long-term exposure raises cancer risk and can affect the brain, heart and eyes. A sudden solar event can deliver a large dose in hours.
When it needs attention
▲ Watch when the daily dose trends above the mission plan. ■ Alert when the rate spikes, which points to a possible solar event. Shelter right away.
